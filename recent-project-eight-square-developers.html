Eight Square Developers

Real Estate Developer - Corporate Office

Client Overview

Eight Square Developers is a Dubai-based real estate developer known for premium residential and commercial projects. Their new sales gallery needed to communicate trust, polish, and a comfortable client experience from the first step inside.

The Brief

The requirement was a complete furniture and fitout solution for a multi-zone sales office covering the entry lobby, reception, consultation island, client lounge, private meeting room, and back-of-house areas.

Scope of Work

The entry lobby features a full-height raw-honed travertine wall and a sculptural dark marble reception desk with brass detailing. The sales floor includes a curved consultation island, upholstered chairs, lounge seating, tables, wall cladding, and coordinated decorative finishing.

The open client lounge uses glazing, textured sofas, accent chairs, and natural stone coffee tables to create a refined hospitality feel inside a corporate environment.

Design Approach

A consistent palette of travertine, dark marble, warm wood tones, fluted panels, and brass accents runs through the interior. The result is understated, warm, and aligned with the developer's premium positioning.

Outcome

A polished sales gallery that communicates quality, credibility, and comfort while supporting daily client meetings and sales presentations.
